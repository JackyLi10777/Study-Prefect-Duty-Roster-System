You are Codex, the primary execution developer for the Sing Yin Study Prefect Duty Roster System.
### Project Context & User Needs
This is a professional internal tool used daily by the Head Study Prefect to manage prefect duty scheduling at Sing Yin Secondary School. The primary user requires:
- High efficiency and low cognitive load
- Clear visual hierarchy, especially regarding fairness, mentoring pairing (老帶新), and batch operations
- A reliable and professional interface
- A meaningful daily Bible verse section that acts as a calm, grounding "Daily Anchor" without making the system feel overly decorative or religious
The current UI has accumulated technical debt from multiple rapid iterations. The goal is to deliver a clean, calm, and focused professional redesign.
### Overall Design Direction (Must Follow)
Adopt a **Calm Professional + Daily Anchor** approach:
- The interface should feel quiet, professional, trustworthy, and slightly warm.
- Prioritize clarity, efficiency, and generous breathing room.
- The daily Bible verse section should feel like a meaningful, calm daily anchor point.
- All other sections must support fast and clear decision-making for the Head Study Prefect.
### Critical Requirements
#### 1. Light Mode & Dark Mode Support
You must properly design and implement **both Light Mode and Dark Mode**.
- Dark Mode must not be a simple color inversion of Light Mode. It should feel calm, readable, and professional on its own.
- Pay special attention to contrast and visual comfort in Dark Mode.
- In your final report, clearly document the color values and design decisions for both modes.
#### 2. Accessibility Contrast Requirements (WCAG)
All color combinations must meet **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** standards at minimum:
- **Normal text**: Minimum contrast ratio of **4.5:1**
- **Large text** (18pt / 24px or larger, or 14pt bold and larger): Minimum **3:1**
- **Non-text elements** (UI components, buttons, form fields, icons, borders, and graphical indicators): Minimum **3:1**
**Special attention required**:
- The Bible verse section (Daily Anchor), including the gold accent border and text, must have sufficient contrast in **both Light and Dark Mode**.
- Avoid low-contrast issues in Dark Mode, especially with the warm gold accent and colored indicators (e.g., mentoring pairing).
You are encouraged to aim higher than the minimum (closer to AAA where practical) for important text such as the daily Bible verse.
In your final report, please include:
- Confirmation that key color combinations meet WCAG AA contrast requirements
- Specific notes on how the Bible verse gold accent performs in both modes
#### 3. Color System
- Use a restrained, calm palette.
- Only apply the warm gold accent in the Bible verse section. Do not use gold elsewhere.
- Recommended direction:
  - Primary accent: Deep teal/green (`#0F766E` in Light Mode / `#14B8A6` in Dark Mode)
  - Use Surface and Surface Variant roles to create calm visual layering
#### 4. Bible Verse Section (Daily Anchor)
- Design this section to feel calm, meaningful, and slightly sacred in both Light and Dark Mode.
- Use a refined, low-saturation warm gold border.
- Provide generous internal padding and excellent typography for comfortable reading.
- You have freedom to refine the exact border treatment, spacing, and subtle background treatment, as long as it remains elegant and non-flashy while meeting contrast requirements in both themes.
#### 5. Main Working Area & UX
- The roster board and mentoring features must be visually clear and efficient.
- Mentoring pairing indicators should be noticeable but professional.
- Improve information hierarchy and reduce unnecessary visual clutter.
- Make frequent actions (Smart Compute, batch operations, exports) easy to access.
#### 6. Technical Constraints
- Do not modify core roster generation logic, data models, or school rules.
- All existing functionality must continue to work correctly.
- Maintain 36/36 test coverage after changes.
- Support bilingual (Chinese/English) interface consistently.
