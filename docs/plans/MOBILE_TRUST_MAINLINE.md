## Ownership and preserved boundaries

- `/platform`: product purpose and one workspace entry. Team, capabilities,
  scenarios, service principles, anonymous operational summary and attribution
  remain available on first use rather than competing on the first screen.
- `/engineering`: one source-bound overall release-evidence summary. The thirteen
  editorial coverage categories are not thirteen independently passed results.
  Filters/table, blueprint, process, principles and evolution are first-use.
- `/system-architecture`: lifecycle and ownership. Detailed stages/layers,
  privacy/recovery explanations, developer commands and FAQ answers are first-use.

Preserve all existing routes, allowlisted outer anchors and reading-lane pager
destinations. Use the D3a opt-in navigation and shared retained expansion helper;
never overwrite NiceGUI's internal expansion IDs. No framework replacement.

Do not modify F2 manifests, evidence loaders/validators, Worker/Viewer, core
models, schema, global styles, audio or deployment state. D3a worktree is frozen.

## Truth and first-use contracts

- Platform always shows release state; an optional passed/total count cannot
  replace stale/fail/missing/unreadable warnings. Keep the existing anonymous
  PlatformSummary projection, never names, roster records, paths or backup files.
- First Platform summary expansion performs one read. Conviction seed and
  attribution image elements are not loaded/mounted until their sections open.
- Engineering loads one overall evidence snapshot per page. All lazy closures
  use that same snapshot; no mixing reports read at different times. Categories
  describe coverage, not individual pass/fail evidence. Actual per-gate evidence
  requires a future explicit manifest/result mapping, outside this batch.
- Unopened detail controls and answer bodies do not exist. Twenty reopens retain
  control identity and filter/view state without repeating reads or starting polls.
- Unavailable data is explicit, with a safe support reference where applicable,
  never converted to fabricated zero counts or a verified state.
- Source-owned TOC/direct links/history reveal before focus, with stale request
  guards and cleanup. Unknown fragments must not be forwarded to the server.
